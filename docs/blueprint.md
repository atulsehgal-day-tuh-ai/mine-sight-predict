# **App Name**: MineSight Predict

## Core Features:

- Risk Ranking Visualization: Display a ranked list of standardized safety risks, highlighting the most critical risks at the top and using a color-coded system to indicate risk levels, enabling quick identification of high-priority areas.
- Risk Trend Analysis: Show the fluctuation of risk rankings over time using line or slope charts, allowing users to select a time range to analyze trends and understand how risks evolve.
- Bow Tie Diagram Generator: Generate interactive Bow Tie diagrams upon selection of a risk, displaying relationships between root causes and critical controls; use visual cues such as color gradients to indicate the probability of impact, facilitating detailed risk assessment.
- AI-Driven Intervention Suggestions: This tool interprets data from predictive models to suggest customized control measures and improvements based on the displayed risk patterns. The LLM assesses which interventions are most likely to effectively mitigate the identified risks, displaying options from a range of interventions.
- Bowtie Image: Provide a secondary visualization focused on risk, control, causas and significant events according with bowtie approach of safety

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep sky blue (#3498db) to convey trust and stability, aligning with the safety focus.
- Background color: Light gray (#ecf0f1), providing a neutral backdrop that enhances readability and focuses attention on the data visualizations.
- Accent color: A vivid shade of orange (#e67e22), positioned approximately 30 degrees to the left of deep sky blue on the color wheel, used sparingly for key interactive elements and calls to action to draw the user's eye without overwhelming the interface. The choice of orange is motivated by its visibility, while ensuring enough differentiation in brightness and saturation to provide clear contrast against the primary color and background.
- Clean, sans-serif fonts for data clarity.
- Intuitive icons to represent risk types and control measures.
- Clear hierarchical layout with risk ranking on the left and detailed Bow Tie diagrams on the right.
- Smooth transitions and subtle animations to enhance user interaction.
